# pc-unwrap

A brand-new PC in its shipping box, and the pleasure of unwrapping it. Pure Luau on the public
`kulisa` API. The box and the PC are Blockbench models rendered with one fixed camera
(`art/pc-unwrap`); the tape, the bag, the films, the glass and the squeegee are live meshes laid
exactly over the renders, the sounds are synthesized.

```
cargo run -p kulisa-player -- demos/pc-unwrap
cargo xtask web --game demos/pc-unwrap --serve 8080      # also ?webgl
cargo xtask apk --game demos/pc-unwrap --abi arm64-v8a,x86_64
```

## What to do

1. **Peel the tape** along the seam where the top flaps meet, then **pull the flaps open**.
2. **Take the top foam caps out** (they come up first, then away) and **lift the PC out** of the
   box by dragging it up. The box goes and the view moves in on the PC.
3. **Pull the bag off.** It is a sleeve over the case with its open end tucked under it, so it only
   comes off one way: up and over the top, while the case stays put (like pulling off a sock).
   Sideways drags only make it lean; let go half way and it slides back down; flick it up or pull
   it most of the way and it comes off, hangs from your hand, and drops when you let go.
4. **Peel the films**: one on the side glass, one on the front glass, and the sticker over the
   I/O on top. Grab the colored tab and pull. The fold rolls the film into a cylinder (the
   *Roll radius* slider changes it), sticks and slips as it peels (a click and a haptic tick each
   time), and a film you let go of keeps its fold: a small peel sticks back down, a big one hangs.
   Peel it all the way and it hangs from your hand; let go and it falls.
5. **Fit a fresh protector** (once the side film is off). It lands with air bubbles trapped under
   it: drag the squeegee over it to iron it flat and push the bubbles to the edges.

Sounds and haptics follow how fast you drag: faster means more and louder crackles.

Keys: `R` starts over, `U` skips the unboxing, `B` whips the bag off, `F3` hides the frame timer.

## How it works

| File | |
|---|---|
| `lib/art.luau` | Generated by `art/pc-unwrap/postprocess.py`: the camera's projection (measured from calibration markers) and where every rendered layer goes. |
| `lib/geom.luau` | Points in centimetres to stage positions, and the faces (side glass, front glass, top) the films live on. |
| `lib/unbox.luau` | The box: layers stacked around the PC (back, PC, near walls, flaps), 12 frames of each flap pair opening, the foam caps, the lift, the zoom. |
| `lib/film.luau` | The peel: fold position, wrap angle, stick-slip, hanging, detaching and falling, all written into mesh buffers each frame (no tables allocated). Header comment has the math. |
| `lib/bag.luau` | The bag as a sleeve with one degree of freedom (how far it is pulled up), wrinkles in material coordinates, gathering into a neck above the case. |
| `lib/protector.luau` | The spare film: landing, bubbles (smooth lenses), squeegee sweep. |
| `lib/prints.luau` | Printed layers drawn once into render targets and used as mesh textures. |
| `lib/sfx.luau` | Speed-dependent crinkle, rustle, squeak and pop playback, haptics. |
| `shaders/plastic.wgsl` | Fresnel, softbox reflection, sharp highlight, underside tint, wrinkle normals, edge glints, curl shadow, bubble lenses. |
| `shaders/glass.wgsl`, `room.wgsl`, `squeegee.wgsl`, `shadow.wgsl` | Smoked glass over the render, the room, the squeegee, film shadows. |

The meshes carry a surface normal per vertex in `data` (read by the shader as `in.data`), and the
shader works out which side of the film you are looking at from the winding of `uv` on screen.
The F3 line shows frames per second and the milliseconds the script spends per frame.

The sounds in `audio/` were synthesized for this demo (filtered noise bursts and tones) and are
released under the repository's MIT license, as is Inter (SIL OFL 1.1, license next to the fonts).
